# Ecology & Wildlife Monitoring

Tutorials for the field-ecology helpers in `omicverse.tl` — open-set
individual re-identification from photographs (photo-ID, camera traps):
re-ID embeddings, geometric verification of candidate pairs against a
random-pair null, a gallery of catalogued animals against new photographs,
and choosing the number of individuals from the data rather than by eye.
The notebook simulates its animals, so the recovered grouping and the
abundance estimate can be scored against the truth.
