# Electrophysiology & Behaviour

Tutorials for the neuroscience helpers in `omicverse.tl` — channel-level
quality control and event-related potentials for multichannel
electrophysiology (EEG), and trial-by-trial computational models of
choice behaviour. Both notebooks simulate their data with a known ground
truth, so every step can be checked, and need nothing beyond NumPy/SciPy.
